# Brand name: "Hop" is close to existing mobility brands

- **Date:** 2026-11-05
- **Status:** Deferred: the user chose to keep "Hop" for now (2026-11-05)
- **Scope:** Product name for the Rider and Captain apps and the operator portal

## Summary

The user noticed "Hop – Enjoy The City", a shared e-scooter app by HOP Teknoloji (Turkey), on the App Store since 2019. A web search found closer conflicts for India:

- **Hopr** (Rapido): a car and bike pooling app launched in 2025 in Bangalore and Delhi. Same country and category, near-identical name. Highest risk.
- **HOP Electric**: an Indian electric-vehicle maker.
- **Hopp** (Bolt Technology): trademark filing covering transportation (not India).

The Indian trademark register was not searched.

## Recommendation

1. Search the IP India Trade Marks Registry for "Hop" in class 39 (transport) and class 9 (software), or ask a trademark lawyer.
2. Decide on a rename before the Play Store release. Today a rename touches app names, Android package ids (`com.hop.rider`, `com.hop.captain`), the wordmark and "h" mark (`style.css` `.brand`, launch art, `ic_hop.xml`), copy in both apps and the operator portal, and the staging service names.
3. Until then, keep new brand-dependent work (the proposed launch animation) with the name and mark defined in one place.

## Open questions

- Keep "Hop" after a trademark check, or rename now?
- If renaming: should the user get a shortlist of names checked against Indian apps and companies?

- **Yaan** (यान, "vehicle", as in Chandrayaan): recommended. Shared Sanskrit root, not tied to one state. Closest existing name: "Yaary" (Hyderabad drivers' association app).
- **Raasta** ("road"): reads as Hindi/Urdu in the south; common word, harder to register.
- **Chalein** ("shall we go?"): strongly Hindi, a risk in Tamil Nadu where the launch starts.
- Tamil-only names (Polaam, Vazhi, Kilambu) were dropped for an India-wide brand. "Polaam" stays as the Tamil text of the main button: one brand, with "let's go" in each language (चलें, వెళ్దాం, ಹೋಗೋಣ, പോകാം, চলো).

Web searches found no ride app or startup with these names; no trademark search has been done.
