What to review

  1. Do the proportions and canopy shape read as a Chennai auto?
  2. Paint, chrome and fabric: realistic enough, or more wear and dust?
  3. Is an original design with no badge right, or do you want to license a real model?
  4. Cost: is the triangle count under the viewer acceptable for one auto? The phone budget is 300k triangles for the whole scene, so traffic autos far away would use a simpler version.

Built in code for this preview (three.js, the same renderer as the game).
