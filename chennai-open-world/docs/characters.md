# Characters: the player and the Chennai cast

Every person in the game is generated by `tools/blender/characters.py` (`npm run blender:characters`)
with MPFB (MakeHuman for Blender) in Blender 5.2 LTS. Nothing is edited by hand: the script is the
source. The people are original: invented faces made from MakeHuman's sliders, no real person's
likeness, and no logos, brands, liveries, insignia or school names on any garment.

## What a person file is

| | Player (`models/player.glb`) | Pedestrian (`models/people/<id>.glb`) | LOD1 (`models/people/<id>_lod1.glb`) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Triangles | at most 30,000 | at most 10,000 | at most 3,500 |
| Textures | 1024 px (two) | 512 px (two) | 512 px in the source, 128 px after `optimize:assets` |
| Materials / draw calls | 2: `Skin` (opaque) and `Outfit` (atlas, alpha-tested for hair cards) | 2 | 2 |
| Body base | full MakeHuman base mesh | MakeHuman low-poly proxy (`male1591`, `female1605`) | the pedestrian's parts, decimated, eyelashes removed |

All files share the same layout:
- One skinned mesh `Body` on MPFB's built-in **mixamo** skeleton: 52 bones named `mixamorig:Hips`,
  `mixamorig:LeftArm`, ... (the game strips the prefix, `packages/player/src/retarget.ts`). The
  LOD1 uses the same skeleton as its LOD0.
- At most four bone weights per vertex, normalised.
- Rest pose: MakeHuman's A-pose (arms down at about 45 degrees). Metres. Feet on the ground at
  the origin (with sandals, the soles are on the ground). Facing glTF +Z (Blender -Y).
- MakeHuman targets (age, sex, proportions, face) are baked: no blend shapes ship.
- Skin hidden under clothes is deleted.

`models/people/people.json` (copied to `optimized/people/people.json`) lists every pedestrian:

```json
{ "people": [ { "id": "man-tshirt-young", "file": "people/man_tshirt_young.glb", "lod1": "people/man_tshirt_young_lod1.glb",
    "sex": "male", "age": "young", "roles": ["pedestrian", "passenger", "college"], "heightM": 1.75,
    "triangles": 9800, "lod1Triangles": 3400, "texture": 512 } ] }
```

`age` is one of `child` (under 13), `teen` (13 to 19), `young` (20 to 35), `middle` (36 to 59),
`elderly` (60 and over). Children are always tagged `child`; the game keeps them out of anything
that can be hurt. `roles` come from: pedestrian, auto-driver, cab-driver, traffic-police, police,
vendor, tea-worker, construction, delivery, shopkeeper, office, college, school, passenger.
`heightM` is the top of the head above the soles of the feet, in the file's own metres.

## How the clothes are made

MakeHuman has western clothes only, so Chennai dress is a mix:

- **MakeHuman garments**, recoloured in the script (the fabric's folds and shading are kept, the
  colour replaced, prints flattened; printed logos on the system T-shirts are painted out):
  shirts and trousers (`male_casualsuit01`), jeans, T-shirts, the banian vest, cargo trousers,
  formal trousers, salwar (`toigo_harem_pants`), the girl's pavadai (`toigo_long_full_skirt`,
  with a woven border added), shoes.
- **Tubes** for wrapped cloth: veshti (off-white with a thin coloured border on the hem and the
  front edge), lungi (an original woven check), saree skirt (front pleats, a broad border and a
  zari line at the hem, small woven motifs), kurta skirt (side slits), the school pinafore skirt.
  Each ring is fitted round the hips and legs; the cloth never narrows over the hips and falls
  in a straight line below them. Weights: hips at the top, each thigh lower down (split
  left/right across the middle), and most of the hem to the shins, so a long garment follows
  the legs when walking.
- **Skin-tight pieces** cut from the body's own surface (faces chosen by the bone that moves
  them, cut cleanly with planes, pushed out by a few millimetres, the skin under them deleted):
  saree blouse, pallu (a band from the right hip over the left shoulder, front and back, with
  borders), kurta top (three-quarter or elbow sleeves), leggings (chudidar), dupatta and towel
  over the left shoulder, head cloth, school shirt and pinafore.
- **Small hard pieces**: plain hard hat, peaked cap, beret (no badge), belt with a plain buckle,
  ID card on a lanyard (blank), sandals (soles and a strap cut from the feet).
- **Skin**: two MakeHuman CC0 skin textures (same UV layout) blended to a brown tone from light
  medium to dark, then a painted moustache (above the upper lip, fitted to the mouth corners),
  stubble or a short beard (jaw, chin and cheeks below the cheekbones), and on some women a small
  bindi.

## Rebuilding

1. Install Blender 5.2 LTS and the MPFB 2.0.18 extension (Edit › Preferences › Get Extensions ›
   Install from Disk, or headless
   `blender --command extension install-file -r user_default add-on-mpfb-v2.0.18.zip`).
2. Unzip `makehuman_system_assets_cc0.zip` into MPFB's user data folder
   (`~/.config/blender/5.2/extensions/.user/user_default/mpfb/data` on Linux), then the
   `clothes/` folders of the community packs above into the same `clothes/` folder. None of this is committed.
3. `BLENDER=/path/to/blender npm run blender:characters` (about 25 minutes for the whole cast; one
   or a few people: `node scripts/blender.mjs tools/blender/characters.py -- assets/models --only man-tshirt-young,woman-saree-young`;
   Cycles review renders with `--preview <dir>`).
4. `npm run optimize:assets`, then commit `assets/models/` and `assets/optimized/`.

To change a person, edit `CAST` in the script (body sliders, tone, hair, facial hair, clothes and
their colours). New garments go in the GARMENT BUILDERS section.

## Known limits

- Faces are MakeHuman faces at 512 px: they read as South Indian at street distance, but close up
  they are smooth and a little doll-like; eyes are the low-poly kind.
- The dupatta and towel are flat bands over one shoulder (no free-hanging cloth); the dupatta
  looks like a panel over half the chest. The pallu is a band from the right hip over the left
  shoulder, front and back, not a hanging drape.
- Long tubes (saree, veshti, ankle lungi): when running, the back shin can push through the hem.
  Walking is fine.
- A kurta over a loose salwar flares more than a real one.
- Hair is MakeHuman's card hair: plaits and short cuts only; buns were dropped (the bun assets
  either carry a non-CC0 licence in their files or looked untidy), so older women wear a plait.
- Police uniforms are generic khaki with plain caps: no insignia, badges or shoulder marks.
- No seated or riding poses are baked here (the game poses the same skeleton).
- The old variants `people/elder.glb`, `lungi.glb`, `tshirt.glb`, `worker.glb` stay only because
  this branch's game code still imports them; delete them once the game reads `people.json`.

## Integration

**Manifest.** `assets/models/people/people.json` lists every person:
`{ id, file, lod1, sex, age, roles, heightM, triangles, lod1Triangles, texture }`, paths
relative to `assets/models/` (the optimised copies, same paths, are under `assets/optimized/`).
`age` is one of child, teen, young, middle, elderly; `roles` are from pedestrian, auto-driver,
cab-driver, traffic-police, police, vendor, tea-worker, construction, delivery, shopkeeper,
office, college, school, passenger. `parsePeopleManifest` (`packages/player/src/people.ts`)
reads it: a broken entry is left out with a console warning, never a crash. 

**Loading** (`apps/game/src/peopleFiles.ts`, `HumanAssets` in `HumanFigure.ts`). Each model is a
lazy `?inline` import (embedded in the one script, TD-3, decoded only when first needed). At the
start the game loads the people who can be pedestrians and drivers; the rest load the first
time someone asks for them (`HumanAssets.look`): police at the first star, the platform crowd
when the player first comes near a station, fare passengers at their stop. Until then a loaded
person of the same cast stands in (enemies are swapped for the right person once loaded). Each
model is loaded once; copies share geometry, textures and clips. Clips are retargeted once per
skeleton (same rig and rest pose: same clips).

**Fit.** Each person is drawn at `heightM` (children and the elderly shorter; the player stays
`PLAYER_HEIGHT`), with the lowest vertex of the rest pose (the soles) on the ground. The walk and
run play faster for shorter legs (stride from hip height relative to the player's), so feet
don't slide. The retargeted clips also lower the hips frame by frame so the lower foot keeps its
rest height, then refine it on each model's own foot vertices so the lowest sole stays at 0
(the clips carry no hip movement; without this walkers floated 2–5 cm and runners about 10 cm).
Browser-tested: soles within 5 cm of the ground standing, walking and running steadily; during
the 0.22 s blend between two clips (a sudden stop) a sole can dip up to about 5 cm.

**Who plays whom** (`CASTS` in `people.ts`; only assignment, nothing about where or how people
move changed):

| Police officers | `police`, `traffic-police` (own uniform) | a grown man dyed khaki |

**Children are never cast where they can be hit, shot or hurt**: pedestrians, enemies, police
and drivers exclude `child` and `teen` (unit-tested against any manifest, and against the real
one when present). Platform crowds are baked figures nothing can touch; fare passengers are not
physics or combat bodies.

**Carrying** (`carryPose.ts`, `HumanFigure.setCarry`, 23 Nov 2026): a box in both hands in
front of the waist, a bag or tiffin carrier in the right hand, or both hands on a trolley's push
bar. A small pose table of arm directions solved on each skeleton like the wave, laid over the
walk cycle; separate from the rest and fight poses. `apps/game/src/goods.test.ts` checks where
the hands land on the player's model.

**Tints.** The player's model keeps its multiply tint (`setClothes` works as before). A manifest
person's outfit is one atlas of coloured garments, where a multiply tint gave muddy colours (a
maroon T-shirt times khaki is brown-red), so tints on manifest people are a dye: the texel's
brightness times the colour. Every garment takes the colour, folds and patterns stay, hair and
shoes stay dark. Gangs keep their colours this way; people dressed for their role get no tint.

**Level of detail.** Animated figures beyond 30 m (2 m hysteresis) show the person's LOD1 and
cast no shadow. 30 m because at the phone's 60° view and 412 px height a 1.7 m person is about
20 px tall there, where a third of the triangles is not visible. LOD1 is the manifest's file,
bound to the same skeleton (one animation for both), or, without one, made at load time with
meshoptimizer's simplifier (35% of the triangles). Seated drivers and passengers are baked
from LOD1. Platform crowds stay baked instanced poses (TD-23), now one set per person.
